STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
SOP-001: HIGHWAY LANE CLOSURE
Traffic Management Center — Version 2.4 | Effective: 2025-01-15
Review Cycle: Annual | Owner: Operations Division

PURPOSE
This procedure establishes the criteria and steps for initiating, managing, and clearing lane closures on monitored highway corridors following an incident or planned maintenance event.

1. ASSESSMENT CRITERIA
Before activating a lane closure, the on-duty Traffic Management Officer (TMO) must confirm at least two of the following conditions:

a) A sensor or camera system reports lane occupancy > 85% on the affected segment for a sustained period of 3+ minutes.
b) Vehicle speed on the affected segment has fallen below 30 km/h against a free-flow baseline of 80+ km/h.
c) A field unit or CAD dispatch has reported a physical obstruction or collision in a travel lane.
d) Camera imagery shows stopped or partially blocking vehicles in one or more lanes.
e) Radar or LIDAR detectors have flagged an anomalous stationary object in the travel lane.

2. ACTIVATION THRESHOLDS
Partial closure (one lane blocked): Immediate activation authorised by TMO on duty.
Full carriageway closure: Requires supervisor approval. Contact Duty Supervisor via radio channel 3 or internal phone extension 201. If supervisor is unreachable within 3 minutes, TMO may act unilaterally when life-safety risk is assessed as imminent.

3. OFFICER RESPONSIBILITIES
Field Unit on Scene:
- Position vehicle 50 m upstream of incident with amber lights active.
- Deploy advance warning signage minimum 200 m upstream.
- Confirm lane status to TMC via radio within 5 minutes of arrival.
- Update TMC every 15 minutes or upon change of conditions.

Traffic Management Officer (TMC):
- Activate VMS boards upstream of closure within 3 minutes of confirmed lane block.
- Coordinate with signal plans team to extend green phases on diversion routes.
- Log closure activation in the Incident Management System (IMS) with timestamp.
- Notify adjacent corridor TMOs of potential spillback.

4. OPENING LANES
A lane may be reopened only when:
a) The field unit confirms the obstruction is fully cleared and the lane surface is safe.
b) Recovery vehicle has departed the scene or is parked fully off the travel lane.
c) No ongoing emergency services activity requires lane exclusion.
d) TMO confirms upstream queue is dissipating before removing VMS messaging.

Reopening sequence: field unit confirms → TMO logs clearance → VMS updated to "LANES CLEAR" for minimum 10 minutes → VMS extinguished.

5. DOCUMENTATION
All closures must be recorded in IMS within 15 minutes of activation. Mandatory fields: corridor ID, lane(s) affected, time of activation, authorising officer, time of clearance. Debrief required within 24 hours for any closure exceeding 90 minutes.
